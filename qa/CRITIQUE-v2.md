# CRITIQUE v2 - independent creative-director review

Reviewed from stills in `qa/review-final/` (desktop1440/1280, tablet768, mobile390/360). I looked at roughly 45 frames and all contact sheets. I did not read the repo.

## 1. Verdict

This is a long way ahead of a typical portfolio, and it has a real signature: one swarm of glossy toy shapes, with page colour changing per chapter. It is not at the lusion / landonorris / bruno-simon bar yet. I would call it plausible for an Awwwards Honorable Mention, and a long shot for Site of the Day. The colour system does its job. Lime, cobalt, vermilion, mint, cyan, violet and pink are all genuinely saturated, and the swarm is mostly full colour on full-bleed colour. The glossy objects in the hero, the dive and the playground are excellent.

The biggest gap is that the middle of the site doesn't deliver on the 3D. The "stories" (cap1, great1, w1, w5) are chains of 360 small objects, packed so densely on a path that they interpenetrate into beaded gunk. They don't read as the agent network, funnel, shield or layered pipeline they claim to be. Lusion's strength is a few big, clean, hero-scale objects with a deliberate silhouette. This site has one very good idea, "a swarm that morphs", and then spends 15 chapters showing the same material as noisy rope.

The second gap is that the last third (archive, edu, xp, contact) drops to pale lavender-white with ghosted or blurred 3D. The brief said bright, full colour, full screen. That is the part where the site stops feeling like the references.

## 3. Top 10 fixes, ranked

1. **Thin out and clean up every "rope" formation (cap1, cap3, great1-3, w1, w5, w6; all viewports).**
   - What is wrong: instances sit so close on the path that they interpenetrate. The rings in cap1, great1 and w1 look like gnarled, beaded cables, and the blue in w1 and w5 collapses into dark navy blobs.
   - What to change: cut the per-formation density by 40-60%. Enforce a minimum spacing of roughly 1.2 times the instance radius. Make the shapes a single type per formation where possible, or sort them by type along the path. This single fix lifts storytelling, material quality and polish together.

2. **Give each formation a bold, unmistakable silhouette (w1, w3, w4, w5, w6; cap1, cap3).**
   - What is wrong: the six project formations are not distinguishable from the copy. w3, w4 and w6 are three horizontal clouds. w5 looks like trousers.
   - What to change: draw them as shapes a viewer can name.
     - w1 LoopGuard: a short chain with one red piece the loop breaks.
     - w3 OULAD: clusters scattered into three visible colour cohorts.
     - w4 microservices: a central hub sphere with spokes.
     - w5 cell tower: a lattice mast with rings radiating out.
     - w6 graph: nodes on a mesh with thin edges.
   - Use fewer, larger instances (maybe 60-120 per chapter) and hide or park the rest.

3. **Make the last third as colourful as the first (archive, edu, xp0-7, tablet and desktop).**
   - What is wrong: the near-white lavender background with ghosted 3D reads as a different site. This is the exact "not full colour" the brief rejects.
   - What to change: give xp a deep cobalt or violet ground with saturated shapes. Give archive and edu their own saturated colours (orange, magenta, teal). Stop reducing the swarm's opacity and saturation in these chapters.

4. **Fix the contact payoff (contact; all viewports).**
   - What is wrong: the swarm is a blur smudge behind the headline, and the frame has no 3D payoff.
   - What to change: bring the swarm back into focus. Place a sharp ring of objects around the headline or at its edges (a lusion-style closing "bursts" frame). Keep the lime background. If the glass backdrop is needed for legibility, clip the blur to the card only.

5. **Resolve the outline-numeral collision (cap1-cap5; desktop1440 and 1280).**
   - What is wrong: the giant "01 / 02 / 04" outline numerals sit on the eyebrow line ("ORCHESTRATION · THE AGENT NETWORK", "RETRIEVAL · THE FUNNEL"), and the strokes cross the text.
   - What to change: move the numeral up so the baseline clears the eyebrow by at least 24px, or push it behind and above the headline block. Fill it with 8% opacity ink instead of an outline if it must sit under text.

6. **Fix the edge aliasing (cap4, cap5, play, w3, w6; all, worst on mobile).**
   - What is wrong: object silhouettes show stair-stepped edges with pink and red pixel fringe and speckles (visible at the edges of the cap2 funnel and in w6). It cheapens the glossy look at full resolution.
   - What to change: enable MSAA or SMAA on the main pass. If you use alpha-to-coverage or dithered transparency, replace it with real MSAA. Cap the pixel ratio so mobile stays smooth. Check for z-fighting on the clearcoat layer.

7. **Make the playground hero copy readable (play; desktop1440, mobile390).**
   - What is wrong: "the swarm." in pink sits on pink and red objects and nearly disappears. "TRY IT" is hidden behind a dark object. The hint box is a translucent teal blob.
   - What to change: put the headline on a flat ink or white plate, or use white text with a subtle shadow. Pin the hint and the "Re-colour everything" button to the bottom with a solid pill background. On mobile, make sure the hint and button are fully inside the frame (the copy is cut off in the capture).

8. **Fix hero composition on mobile and tablet (hero; mobile390, mobile360, tablet768).**
   - What is wrong: the swarm is washed out and translucent behind the headline. CHARBEL is flush with the right edge, with zero gutter. There is a large empty gap between the swarm and the name.
   - What to change: reduce the name size slightly or tighten letter-spacing so it keeps a 20px gutter. Move the swarm down into the empty gap at full opacity, and keep the headline and swarm from overlapping.

9. **Stop the nav from being overlapped by objects (cap4, w5, play, great2; desktop).**
   - What is wrong: swarm pieces pass under the mono nav links, which makes SYSTEMS / GREAT / WORKS / EXPERIENCE hard to read (cap4, w5).
   - What to change: keep the 3D inside a safe margin under the nav (about 90px). Alternatively add a subtle nav backdrop or gradient scrim.

10. **Make the dive transition feel like a full-screen event (dive; all).**
    - What is wrong: the frame is a small blurred glass panel on blue, and the headline is good, but the panel's backdrop blur turns the swarm into mud. The best objects of the whole site are pushed to the edges.
    - What to change: remove the heavy blur, put the type directly over a frame where the swarm explodes outward, and let the shapes pass in front of and behind the headline for depth. That is the Lusion / bruno-simon moment this site is missing.

## 4. Things that look broken

- **Outline numerals colliding with the eyebrow text** in cap1, cap2, cap4 and cap5 on desktop (strokes cross "THE AGENT NETWORK", "THE FUNNEL", "THE DISTRIBUTION").
- **Edge aliasing and fringe** on almost every object edge, most visible on mobile (cap4, cap5, play) and on w3/w6 desktop. Stair-steps and stray single pixels.
- **Interpenetrating instances** in cap1, great1, great3, w1, w2 and w5. They look like a rendering error and not a design choice.
- **w5 formation** reads as trousers, with a rainbow cap at the top that runs under the nav.
- **Nav overlapped by objects** in cap4, w5 and play.
- **Mobile play** copy is cut off at the bottom of the frame ("anywhere to burst it open." is truncated, and the button is not visible).
- **Mobile hero** has CHARBEL touching the right edge, and the eyebrow wraps over swarm shapes.
- **Cap1 (desktop and mobile)** swarm is cropped by the left edge of the viewport. About a third of the green ring is off-screen.
- **Contact** swarm is a blur smudge, effectively an empty 3D frame.
- **xp7 (tablet and mobile)** has half the frame empty above and below a small floating column of shapes. This frame was captured before the helix fix, as noted.
- **Great2 (desktop)** is a white rope cropped off the left edge, with no labels for its layers.

## 5. What I cannot judge from stills

- Scroll choreography: whether the morphs between formations are smooth or pop, and how long each transition takes.
- Whether the swarm's motion (drift, wobble, flocking) feels alive or jittery. Stills can't show the rope clumping at speed.
- Playground interaction: cursor push, click-burst, and the recolour animation.
- Frame rate and perceived performance on mid-range laptops and phones, especially the 360-instance transmission materials.
- Whether the aliasing is a screenshot artefact (device pixel ratio 1) or is also visible on a retina display.
- Hover and focus states on cards, CTAs and nav, and any custom cursor.
- Loading experience and sound, if any.
- The xp helix after it was opened up.

---

**Summary reply**

- Overall: 6.5/10. A strong, colourful signature, but not yet Awwwards SOTD level.
- Fix 1: thin the instance density in every formation so shapes stop interpenetrating.
- Fix 2: redraw the project formations (w1, w3-w6) as bold, nameable silhouettes.
- Fix 3: bring full saturated colour and sharp 3D into xp, archive and edu.
- Fix 4: make contact a sharp 3D payoff, not a blur.
- Fix 5: fix the outline-numeral / eyebrow collision, the edge aliasing, and the play-headline legibility.
