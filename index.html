07 FAQ

What type of AI engineering projects do you specialize in?

I specialize in production-grade LLM applications: multi-agent autonomous systems, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) architectures, automated LLM evaluation frameworks (like GREAT), MCP server integrations, and full-stack AI product development.

Are you available for full-time roles, contract work, or advisory?

Yes! I am open to full-time AI engineering positions, targeted contract engagements, and high-impact advisory projects across US, UK, and EU time zones.

How do you prevent AI hallucinations and ensure reliability in production?

I implement multi-layered safeguards: structured output schemas, vector grounding, state-cycle circuit breakers (such as LoopGuard), and automated LLM-as-a-judge regression benchmarks before any deployment.

What is your guaranteed response time for inquiries?

I guarantee a response within 24 hours for all professional inquiries submitted via email or LinkedIn (typically responding in less than 4 hours during business hours).

How do you handle data privacy and security compliance?

All AI architectures strictly comply with GDPR and privacy standards. Client data is handled with zero-data-retention APIs, isolated VPC vector stores, and robust encryption.
