Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

1. Overview

This policy explains how data is handled when you visit charbelto.github.io, the portfolio of Charbel Toumieh, AI Engineer. In short: this site does not track you.

2. Analytics and tracking

This site does not use analytics. It does not load Google Analytics or any other analytics, advertising, social-media or fingerprinting script, and it contains no tracking pixels.

3. Cookies and storage

This site sets no cookies and does not write to your browser's local storage or session storage, so there is no cookie banner. Your browser keeps its normal cache of the site's files, as it does for any web page.

4. Third-party requests

All fonts, scripts and 3D rendering code are served from this site itself; no third-party font or script services are contacted. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may process technical request data (such as your IP address) as the host, under its own privacy statement. Links to GitHub, LinkedIn and project repositories take you to third-party sites that have their own policies.

5. Direct contact

There is no contact form. If you email charbeltoumieh1@gmail.com or message through a linked platform, the details you provide (such as your name, address and message) are used only to reply to you and to discuss professional opportunities.

6. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of any correspondence you have sent. Email charbeltoumieh1@gmail.com.
